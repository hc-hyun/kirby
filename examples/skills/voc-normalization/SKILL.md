# VOC normalization

1. Identify the symptom, affected feature, and reproduction conditions.
2. Keep unknown attributes unknown and separate customer words from interpretation.
3. Consult references/demo-taxonomy.md for the synthetic demonstration categories.
4. Preserve input and evidence identifiers. Do not infer prevalence from one report.

This is a demonstration taxonomy, not a production quality standard.
